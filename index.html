Why this matters

Urban rivers are living systems

Rivers flowing through towns and cities can be affected by roads, land use, rainfall, and human activity. These pressures can influence both water quality and the organisms living in the river.

This website communicates iron data from River Medlock macroinvertebrates using maps, charts, short explanations, and interactive filters for residents, students, and community groups.

Communication goal

Make environmental monitoring easier to understand by connecting scientific data to local places and familiar river life.

How the data was collected

Sampling across Summer and Autumn 2024

Benthic macroinvertebrates were collected using a 3-minute kick-net sampling technique. Sampling occurred between June and November 2024, covering Summer and Autumn.

The website keeps the sampling explanation short because its main purpose is communication, not presenting a full experimental report.

Season guide

  • Summer: June to August
  • Autumn: September to November
  • Focus: communicating seasonal patterns simply
